# 📱 Mobile App Market Research

This project analyzes real behavioral data from mobile game players to understand engagement, retention, and monetization. It includes retention analysis, evaluation of A/B test results for promotional offers, and KPI development for time-limited game events. The focus is on identifying mechanics and offer types that improve user experience and drive revenue.

## 🎯 Goal

Conduct a product analytics study using real user behavior data from a mobile game. The focus is on evaluating player retention, the performance of A/B tested promotional offers, and the effectiveness of a time-limited in-game event. The results will support product decisions aimed at maximizing engagement and revenue.

---

## ✅ Objectives

1. **Retention Analysis:**  
  Analyze daily player retention from their registration date. Develop a flexible Python function that works efficiently on both full and sampled datasets.

2. **A/B Test Evaluation:**  
  Evaluate performance of A/B-tested promotional offers using key revenue metrics (ARPU, conversion rate). Determine whether the test group’s uplift is statistically and practically significant.

3. **Event Performance Metrics:**  
  Develop KPIs to assess in-game event success, considering both standard and penalty-based variations. Focus on engagement, reward acquisition, and user progression.

## 📊 Key Results

Below are the key results from each analytical goal within the project.

### 🎯 Goal 1: Retention Analysis

- **Day 1 Retention**: ~2% - indicates high early churn.
- **Day 6 Spike**: ~7–8% - likely triggered by scheduled game events.
- **Long-tail Retention**: stabilizes after Day 7 at a low, steady level.

### 🎯 Goal 2: A/B Test Evaluation

- The number of paying users is similar across both groups.
- Group B outperformed across all key revenue metrics:
  - **ARPU**: B - 26.75 vs. A - 2.7
  - **ARPPU**: B - 3003.66 vs. A - 302.46
  - **Total Revenue**: B - 5.42M vs. A - 0.55M
- Z-score filtering was used to remove major outliers in group A.
- The uplift in group B is strong enough to justify rollout without further testing.

### 🎯 Goal 3: Event Evaluation

- **Retention**: during-event participation and post-event activity.
- **Engagement**: participation and completion rates.
- **Monetization**: total revenue and ARPU.
- **Operational metrics** were introduced for complex mechanics (e.g., retries, time-to-complete, drop-offs).
- The framework enables consistent event comparisons and informs tuning of future mechanics.

### 🔍 Business Relevance

These findings provide actionable insight for optimizing user retention, scaling promotional offers, and tuning in-game event mechanics to improve both engagement and revenue.
